August 2025

🌟 The Compassionate Future Summit 2025 – Was Great! 🌟

While I fell ill right after the event and am still recovering, I need to share how amazing our weekend was!

From 21–25 August 2025, we gathered at “Jugenddorf am Müggelsee, Berlin” for the Compassionate Future Summit 2025 – a participant-driven working and community weekend dedicated to reducing and preventing suffering across sentient life and long timelines.

We had 45 ambitious participants from 17 countries, including from 6 countries outside Europe! 

Building on the success of the 2019 Suffering-Focused Ethics Retreat in Switzerland, this year’s Compassionate Future Summit succeeded in attracting and nurturing amazing talent, connecting people, projects, and ideas, and fostering a resilient community culture.

Through Preparation & Content Contribution Calls we encouraged research, content and self development ahead of the event:

  • Exploring, discussing and co-developing ideas how event attendance can support both the individual’s impact goals and those of the other event attendees. 
  • Reflecting on what cause area(s) attendees want to work on or how they want to optimise their career for impact. Exploratory and rough, but aiming to include:
    • A theory of change — for one’s career trajectory, projects or potential content contribution, ranking strategies by potential impact and risk/benefit ratio
    • Long-term projections (evolution, obstacles, risks and benefits) — how one’s approach might impact future sentient beings in the next 10, 50, & 200 years

The event featured a wide range of talks, workshops, and collaborative sessions, complemented by structured social activities, reflective practices, and community-building exercises.

I’m deeply grateful to everyone who contributed — in preparation calls, in content sessions, and in countless informal conversations. The energy, openness, creativity and compassion you brought made the weekend what it was.

A more detailed report (with highlights and takeaways) will follow soon. And we’ve started organising next year’s Summit (August 2026, probably Berlin again) — so stay tuned!

Also, our online working & community meetups need to be revived soon - Stay tuned for a refreshed format!

March 2025

Updates on the Compassionate Future Summit 

Two Exceptional Guests Confirmed

We’re absolutely thrilled to welcome two remarkable guests whose work has transformed how we think about suffering, ethics, and our responsibilities in the world:

David Pearce – Keynote Speaker

British utilitarian philosopher and co-founder of Humanity+, David Pearce is among the most influential thinkers in suffering-focused ethics. His manifesto, The Hedonistic Imperative, lays out a bold vision for using science and technology to abolish suffering across all sentient life. In it, he argues that this goal is not only ethically urgent, but also technically feasible—through the responsible use of biotechnology and genetic engineering.

David’s idea of a “compassionate biosphere” challenges us to expand the boundaries of moral progress—and inspires bold, practical action to reduce suffering in humans and non-humans alike. We’re deeply honoured to host him.

Jack Hancock-Fairs – Special Guest

Widely known for his compelling YouTube channel Humane Hancock and podcast Humane Hangouts, viewed and listened to by millions, Jack Hancock-Fairs explores urgent topics such as animal ethics, veganism, and wild animal suffering. His work stands out for its rare blend of clarity, emotional depth, and disarming humour.

At the Summit, Jack will be sharing a glimpse of a deeply personal project he's been working on for a number of years.

🌱 Meanwhile, our content contribution calls with participants are in full swing. A wide range of talks, workshops, and collaborative sessions are taking shape, and we look forward to sharing a preview of the rich program in the coming weeks.
