Call for Community Support

We are currently facing several setbacks, including the loss of funding for the upcoming Compassionate Future Summit and significant administrative constraints around attendee cost coverage.

To enable future iterations of the Summit, we need either:

  • A fiscal sponsor willing to support a less restrictive event setup (including allowing commitment deposits), or
  • Access to a non-profit German tax accountant with expertise in non-profit entrepreneurship and event accounting

In addition, approximately €5,000 in annual funding would help cover a minimal share of our operational costs. Note, that to this point our work is conducted on a fully voluntary basis and to a large extent self-funded. 

We will continue our efforts to resolve these hurdles and are confident that, with the help of the community, we will be able to continue the Summits in the future.

If you are able and willing to support us financially, legally, or administratively, we would be grateful to hear from you.

2025 Annual Report 

2025 Annual Report Compassionate Future Initiative

In 2025 we expanded our community building strategies around Suffering-Focused Ethics (SFE), covering some major neglected cause areas and developing our community support portfolio. 

Key highlights include:

  • Hosting the Compassionate Future Summit 2025 in Berlin, bringing together 45 participants from 17 countries for a participant-driven working and community event focused on long-term suffering reduction.
  • Active outreach and community engagement at major EA events, including EAG London, EAGx Berlin, and EAGx Amsterdam, with multiple SFE meetups and satellite sessions.
  • Developing and promoting our impact oriented guidelines. 
  • Publishing and advancing Compassionate Genomics, outlining genomic welfare and moral predispositions as underexplored, high-impact intervention areas.
  • Strengthening ties within the growing SFE ecosystem, alongside organisations such as the Center for Reducing Suffering and Sentient Futures.
  • Organisational consolidation through rebranding to the Compassionate Future Initiative and expansion of the website and collaboration pathways.

Looking ahead, 2026 will focus on professionalising our organisational infrastructure, updating content such as the interventions mind map with up-to-date opportunities and delivering the next Compassionate Future Summit in Berlin.

 

2024 Annual Report

2024 Annual Report International Suffering Abolitionists

The 2024 Annual Report reflects our continued engagement in community building, research, and advocacy. Key highlights include:

  • Hosting online meetups and in-person events like EAG London and Berlin.

  • Promoting genetic and technological solutions to reduce suffering.

  • Expanding awareness through online platforms such as newsletters and TikTok.

  • Strengthening connections with organisations like CLR and CRS.

Looking ahead, 2025 will see us hosting the Compassionate Future Summit in Berlin and furthering our mission through various initiatives.

Other Plans for 2025

  • Monthly Online Meetups

We will continue hosting our monthly Online Working / Community Meetup for the Abolition of Suffering. Stay updated by following us on the EA Forum for announcements about upcoming (online) events.

  • EAG London 2025

We plan to participate in EAG London 2025 and explore opportunities to contribute through meetups and workshops.

 

Past in-person events

  • EAG London (June 2025),  EAGxBerlin (October 2025), EA Global London (May/June 2024), EAGxBerlin (September 2024), EAGxRotterdam (November 2022) and the the International Animal Rights Conference (September 2023) contributing with workshops, meetups, or lightning talks.
